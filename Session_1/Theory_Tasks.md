## Task 1: What is NLP?
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on giving computers the ability to understand, interpret, and generate human language in a way that is both meaningful and useful. A great daily example is WhatsApp's auto-complete and predictive text feature, which uses NLP to understand the context of what you are typing and suggest the next logical word in your sentence.

## Task 2: Importance of NLP in AI Apps
1.  **Chatbots (e.g., Swiggy/Zomato Customer Support):** NLP allows the AI to understand user complaints or requests written in natural, messy human language (like "my food is late") and instantly categorize the issue to provide automated refunds or connect to a live agent, drastically reducing wait times.
2.  **Spam Filters (e.g., Gmail):** NLP constantly analyzes the text inside incoming emails to detect deceptive patterns, urgent language, or scam keywords, automatically filtering them out so the user's main inbox remains clean and secure.
3.  **Search Autocomplete (e.g., YouTube/Google):** NLP predicts what the user is trying to search for based on the first few letters or words, correcting spelling mistakes on the fly and saving the user significant time and effort.
